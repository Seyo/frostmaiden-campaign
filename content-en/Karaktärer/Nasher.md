Elf paladin, Order of Tyr (Avenger — show no mercy to the wicked). From Neverwinter. High charisma and a strong moral compass. Speaks Dwarvish. Carries a grappling hook and javelins.

- Fell in battle during the wolf attack (Day 6) and again against [[Nildar Sunblight(†)]] (Day 18).
- Killed [[Eevie(†)]] in the fortress cell block in self-defense (fungus-infected) — carries the guilt. See [[Nashers misstag]]. Wrapped her in his own blanket and carried her out with reverence — shed a tear, the first time since Letharia was executed.
- Refuses to let [[Zahir]] desecrate corpses or take shortcuts at the expense of others' dignity. Grudgingly concedes his value when the charm delivers — "He's actually pretty useful sometimes."
- Found a **business ledger** in the Sunblight fortress on Day 19 proving that [[Glenn]] (*The Hook, Line and Sinker*) has been buying ale from the Sunblight dwarves for roughly 2 years. Confronted [[Jarthra]] directly — she is not the buyer (stopped delivering to Glenn about 2 years ago, matching the ledger). No confrontation with Glenn yet.
- Forgot the goats in the fortress cages. Realized it all the way back in Caer Konig: *"DAMN IT! WE FORGOT THE GOATS!"* (Day 19).
- Was deeply disturbed by [[Zahir]]'s and [[Jarthra]]'s discussion about life being better when people were sacrificed to [[Auril]], during the journey back to Caer Konig.
- Spent the rest period (Days 19–23) preaching about Tyr to the townsfolk, and the evenings getting to know the others at the tavern — now knows more about Zahir's and Sork's pasts.
- Is aware of **Dzaan** — asked the innkeeper at [[Dvärgadalen]] about him (Day 25); she claimed not to know the name, but was obviously lying.
- Walked straight up to the unknown dwarves (Hruna, Korux, and Storn) in the cave north of [[Dvärgadalen]] (Day 24) and introduced himself — turned out to be friendly, not Duergar.
- Healed [[Sork the Shadow]] after the avalanche on Day 26 — much appreciated. Also healed [[Garret]] after the gang found him badly wounded in the snow.
- Nearly killed by the mother yeti's ice block during the bridge retreat (Day 26). Ran back across the bridge under fire anyway to drag the unconscious Sork to safety.
- Stabilized [[Perilou Fishfinger]] with his medkit during the retreat.
- Traded his Greatsword for a **Longsword + sturdy shield** in Termalaine (Days 29–33) — painted Tyr's symbol on the shield.
- Failed to make contacts in Termalaine through carousing (Days 29–33).
- Cast **Divine Sense** (Day 34) and immediately sensed that [[Throgga Icevein(†)]] was not alive. Confronted Throgga directly: _"You cannot fool a holy paladin of Tyr. I know exactly what you are. Undead. Speak now."_
- Briefly affected by the wraith's terrifying gaze during the battle, but shook it off. Called on Tyr's healing power to revive the unconscious Sork mid-fight.
- Estimated that [[Throgga Icevein(†)]] had been **dead for approximately 2.5 years** — exactly since the Rime began. The connection to the murder investigation confirmed. Hildur was right.
- Confronted **Kendra** (employee at Throgga's Import & Goods) with: *"YOU'RE UNEMPLOYED. YOUR BOSS IS DEAD."* — thrown out. Day 35.
- Triggered a magical lightning trap on a hidden cash box at Throgga's — blessed Sork for the next attempt, but Sork was also hit (twice). Third attempt succeeded. Day 35.
- Found **Fronda Harroway** hanged in the foreman's house and saved **Arno** from the forest with Lay on Hands. Day 37.
- Revealed **his wings and transformed eyes** to the party during a kobold fight in the new mine — companions very surprised. Day 38.
- Examined **Gillian Fieldspar**: died from the fall in the lift shaft; skin and flesh cut from arms and legs post mortem. Day 38.
- Received a **vision**: his mother offers a child to an unknown god — the feeling that the child was his brother, a brother he does not have. Day 38.
- Cast **Bless** during the spore release to protect the others — inhaled the spores himself; used Lay on Hands to cure himself. Day 38.
- Used his last Lay on Hands on Borc after the second mushroom body. Day 38.
- Dreamed of his mother at an altar — praying in an unknown language before a **white skull symbol**. The sense of a dark god. Days 38–39.
- Healed Borc with Healing Word after Sork shoved him into the lift shaft. Day 39.
- Overwhelmed by **shame and guilt** outside House of the Triad — the infection amplifying his guilt over leaving the church in Neverwinter. Forced to admit to the others that he **was not on the temple's good side** and that this was part of why he is in the north. Day 39.
- Held in a cage for five days (Days 40–44) while House of the Triad healed the magical affliction.
- Asked the monks about the **white skull symbol** (Day 45) — they did not recognize it. Offered access to the temple library for future research.
- Cast **Detect Poison and Disease** behind the secret door — sensed strong necromantic presence; identified the source of the infection. Day 45.
- Bound **Mari Flatstream** (half-transformed lieutenant) — her gaze met his and *"it felt as if something died in me."* Day 45.
- Poured **Holy Water** on Cadavix — no effect (neither undead nor fiend). Day 45.
- Identified the Loross text using his Elvish (Sork contributed Draconic). Day 45.
- Mentioned **Dzaan** and the **Arcane Brotherhood** as possible sources on Netheril — refused to explain to Borc how he knew of them. Day 45.
- Took the unconscious **Mari Flatstream** out of the mine — hopes she can be cured now that Cadavix is gone. Day 45.

## Background

Trained at **The Hall of Justice** in Neverwinter to become a Justiciar of Tyr — Tyr's arm on earth, sent to uphold justice and protect the innocent.

Something happened in Neverwinter. He **left the church** and is in the north partly because of it — exactly what occurred is still unknown. The magical infection in the mine amplified his guilt to breaking point outside House of the Triad.
