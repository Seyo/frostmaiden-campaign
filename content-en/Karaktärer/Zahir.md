Halfling warlock, from Calimport in southern Faerûn. Sharp tongue, high charisma, morally questionable. Fleeing something in Calimport. Extraordinarily lucky.

- Runs "performances" for his "crew" in towns — exactly what this entails remains unclear.
- Spell: Tasha's Hideous Laughter (defeated [[Nildar Sunblight(†)]]).
- Negotiated with [[Jarthra]] and [[Throgga Icevein(†)]] — seems skilled at driving down prices.
- Proposed blowing up [[Aubril(†)]]'s body with magic — [[Nasher]] put a stop to it.
- Found an alchemy lab in the Sunblight fortress on Day 19 with a **glowing fungus** in an uncapped glass flask. Left it alone — later confirmed by [[Foxes in the Snow]] that the spores likely come from the **Underdark** and are dangerous to inhale. The right call.
- Discussed old Auril sacrifices with [[Jarthra]] on the journey back from the fortress — seemed genuinely curious whether life was better when humans were offered up to her (Day 18–19). [[Nasher]] finds the thought horrifying.
- Spent the evenings of the rest period (Days 19–23) drinking with [[Trovus]].
- Identified **Hood of Water Breathing** (speak a command word → bubble allows underwater breathing) and **Potion of Healing** during the rest week.
- Blew his stealth approaching the goblin camp north of [[Dvärgadalen]] (Day 24) and triggered the fight.
- Rolled a **nat 20** on _Speak with Animals_ against the furious polar bears: *"STOP — FRIENDS — KILL"* — turned **2 polar bears** into allies during the goblin fight.
- After the fight, talked the bears into hauling the dwarves' iron ore back. Then offered the bears to the dwarves as a thank-you gift — they politely declined two enormous predators. The bears were released and looked very happy.
- Used _Speak with Animals_ on [[Garret]]'s lead sled dog at the abandoned camp — the dog was fixated on finding its "Master," confirming [[Garret]] was up the mountain.
- Nearly killed in the avalanche on Day 26.
- Learned what an **Opportunity Attack** is the hard way against the crag cats on Day 26 — the cat missed, so the lesson came cheap.
- Suspected [[Asterix(†)]]'s leather-bound spellbook was trapped — pocketed it without opening it. Sold it in Termalaine for **175 gp** (Days 29–33).
- Stabilized [[Borc the Orc]] and [[Sork the Shadow]] with Spare the Dying during the yeti retreat (Day 26). Set up an alarm spell at the mountain camp. Hurled Eldritch Blasts at the mother yeti from across the bridge gap.
- Accidentally ruined the drinks in the brewing kit at the mountain camp — contributed to the party running short of food and water during the extra night.
- Tried to smooth things over with silver-tongued diplomacy when [[Nasher]] confronted [[Throgga Icevein(†)]] (Day 34) — Throgga was unmoved and attacked.
- Calmed the guard dogs at Throgga's Import & Goods with **Speak with Animals** before the night break-in. Day 35.
- Helped fetch **Elka Verryt** to calm Arno after he was rescued from the forest. Day 37.
- Set up **alarm spells** at the mine entries — Nasher suspects he may have done something else at the first one (Day 38).
- Repeatedly stared at Nasher inside the mine — Nasher noticed and does not trust him. Day 38.
- Believed he could tell a beam had been sabotaged to cause the collapse that killed Gror Gersk — seemed overconfident. Day 38.
- Came too close to the mushroom body and was nearly struck by the mushrooms (Day 38).
- Was the second person (after Sork) to see the shadow move. Day 38.
- **Forced the entire party into House of the Triad** (Day 39) when they could barely cross the threshold themselves — without Zahir they would not have gone in.
- Knew that **Loross** had been widely used in the **Netherese Empire** — identified the language on the plaque as belonging to the ancient, extinct magical empire. Day 45.
- Observed that the chamber behind the secret door had once been **open to the sky like a crater** — proposed the crystal egg had come from above. Day 45.
- Threw a **blanket over Mari Flatstream** to protect the party from her gaze. Day 45.
- Attempted to cast **Phantasmal Force** on the hook horror — failed. Day 45.
- Buried under a **cave-in** caused by the small clawed creature on the upper floor (Day 45) — took one hour to dig out.
- Set an alarm spell at the lift shaft on the upper floor. Day 45.
- What **the egg's influence** planted in Zahir's head remains unknown.
- Helped Sork leave Bryn Shander during their first week in Icewind Dale — has never gone into details.
