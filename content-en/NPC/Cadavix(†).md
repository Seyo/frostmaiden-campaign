title: "Cadavix"
---

**Faction:** Netherese Empire (historical)

**High Necromancer Cadavix (III).** Body with translucent white skin, coated in diamond dust — skeleton and organs visible through the flesh. The plaque around his neck is in **Loross** (ancient Netherese language).

> **3516 NY = −343 DR → 1,835 years old** (found in 1492 DR)

- Was sealed inside a colossal blue crystalline egg on the lowest level of [[Termalaine]]'s new mine. The egg likely fell from above — the chamber was once open to the sky like a crater.
- The crystalline egg emitted necrotic energy that infected all mine staff (hardened nails, madness, hallucinations). The infection spread gradually toward Termalaine.
- Borc and Nasher were charmed by the egg and compelled to harm themselves. Sork pulled them both free.
- The egg planted thoughts in the party's minds (including *"[[Arno]] must die. The skull demands a sacrifice. Tonight."*).
- [[Mari Flatstream]] (lieutenant) became completely obsessed with protecting the egg — half-transformed into a monster.

**† Day 45:** Borc struck the egg with a single pickaxe blow — it split in two. The body was found inside. Borc threw Cadavix into a shaft leading toward the Underdark — no sound was heard from below. The oppressive feeling vanished.

**Note:** Thoughts planted by the egg lingered in the party afterward. The white skull symbol from Nasher's dreams — the monks in [[Bryn Shander]] did not recognise it. The temple's library and the **Arcane Brotherhood** (Dzaan mentioned) are possible sources on Netheril.
