---
title: "Fronda Harroway"
---

**Faction:** [[Ten-Towns]] (Termalaine)

Dwarf, foreman of [[Termalaine]]'s new mine. Adoptive mother of **[[Arno]]**.

- Closed the new mine around Day 21 after monsters came up from underground.
- Sent letters to Speaker [[Oarus Matthews]]: the first demanded closure and that [[Gillian Fieldspar(†)]] be sent down; the second appeared to be written by someone losing their mind — the handwriting deteriorating.
- Left a final message for [[Arno]] on her bedroom door: *"I'm sorry"*.

**† ~Day 32–33:** Found hanged in the foreman's house at the mine by [[Zahirs gäng]] (Day 37). Suicide, 4–5 days old. Swollen, glassy eyes; hardened nails (symptoms of [[Cadavix(†)]]'s infection). Clutched a small bone bead in her hand.
