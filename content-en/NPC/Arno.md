---
title: "Arno"
---

**Faction:** [[Ten-Towns]] (Termalaine)

Mute boy, orphaned (Tribe of the Wolf). Adopted by mine foreman [[Fronda Harroway(†)]].

- Was in Termalaine when the new mine was closed.
- Found without a pulse in a ramshackle hut in the forest outside the new mine (Day 37) — saved by [[Nasher]]'s Lay on Hands.
- Carried back to warmth; no real contact could be established initially. [[Elka Verryt]] managed to calm him and brought him back to town.
- Current whereabouts: [[Termalaine]] (in [[Elka Verryt]]'s care).
