---
title: "Elka Verryt"
---

**Faction:** [[Ten-Towns]] (Termalaine)

Secretary to Speaker [[Oarus Matthews]] in [[Termalaine]].

- Was at Town Hall when [[Zahirs gäng]] searched for Oarus (Day 36). Confirmed that the troubles in the new mine were real and that it was not the Speaker who had closed it.
- Was persuaded that the party was there to help — revealed that Oarus was at Sune's Comfort.
- Managed to calm **[[Arno]]** and brought him back to safety in town (Day 37) when the party found him near death outside the new mine.
