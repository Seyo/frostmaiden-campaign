---
title: "Gillian Fieldspar"
---

**Faction:** [[Ten-Towns]] (Termalaine)

Dwarf jeweller. Owned **Fieldspar Jewels** in [[Termalaine]] (originally from Dwarven Valley). Closed the shop around Day 26 — reason unknown.

- Foreman [[Fronda Harroway(†)]]'s letter to Speaker [[Oarus Matthews]] demanded that Gillian be sent down into the new mine.
- **† ~Days 28–33:** Found dead at the bottom of the elevator shaft in the new mine by [[Zahirs gäng]] (Day 38). Died from the fall. Skin and flesh cut away from arms and legs with a blade — post mortem. Two diamonds (8 gp each) left on the body.
- Shop has been closed since ~Day 26. The party scouted the exterior on their return to Termalaine but noticed nothing unusual.
