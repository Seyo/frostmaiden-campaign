---
title: "Perilou Fishfinger"
---

**Faction:** Independent (former [[The Burning Hammers]])

Halfling, priestess of **Yondalla** — the matriarch, goddess of protection and fertility. Lawful good. Yondalla's symbol: a cornucopia on a shield.

- Was with [[The Burning Hammers]] (led by her husband [[Mokingo Akannathi(†)]]) heading to [[Termalaine]] to investigate the murders.
- Captured by a yeti mother in the cave system at [[Kelvins Cairn]] (Day 26) — kept alive as a "toy" for the cub.
- Rescued by an invisible [[Sork the Shadow]] who crept past the yeti, gave her a healing potion, and led her out along a rocky path.
- Granted the party Yondalla's blessing during the fight against the father yeti.
- Revealed on Day 28: all murder victims had a **snowflake carved into the left shoulder**; the murders coincided with deliveries from [[Throgga Icevein(†)|Throgga's Import & Goods]].
- Mokingo was her husband → devastated by his death.
- Did not want to adventure further after the mountain. Taken to [[Termalaine]] by [[Jarthra]].

**† Days 34–35:** Found half-naked and dead in the snow outside Termalaine's city wall. **"You owe me"** carved into her chest — murdered by freezing (stripped and doused with cold water until the cold took her life). Borc was falsely accused; the party was cleared by guard captain **[[Morg Ashbone]]**.
