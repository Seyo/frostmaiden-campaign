title: "Mari Flatstream"

**Faction:** [[Ten-Towns]] (Termalaine)

Dwarf, lieutenant at [[Termalaine]]'s new mine.

- One of the mine's seven lieutenants under foreman [[Fronda Harroway(†)]]. Was in the mine when it was closed.
- Became completely obsessed with protecting [[Cadavix(†)]]'s crystalline egg — half-transformed into a creature with long claws and sharp teeth (the same type that tried to kill Zahir with a collapsed mine wall).
- Convinced that everything was **Menka Bladeless**'s fault.
- **Day 45:** Borc took her down; Nasher bound her. Her gaze met Nasher's — *"something died in him"*. Zahir threw a blanket over her face; Nasher knocked her unconscious.
- Taken out of the mine by the party — may be curable.

**Current condition and whereabouts unknown.**
