---
title: "Oarus Matthews"
---

**Faction:** [[Ten-Towns]] (Termalaine)

Speaker (leader) of [[Termalaine]].

- Last seen at The Blue Clam (~Day 33) — got drunk and disappeared. Believed he had been drugged or poisoned there.
- Was hiding at the bathhouse **Sune's Comfort**. Password: *"Executive Special"*.
- Found by [[Zahirs gäng]] on Day 36 via a tip from [[Elka Verryt]].
- Confirmed that the troubles in the new mine were real and that it was not he who had closed it.
- Showed letters from foreman [[Fronda Harroway(†)]]: the first demanded closure and that [[Gillian Fieldspar(†)]] be sent down; the second appeared to be written by someone losing their mind — the handwriting deteriorating.
- Promised his support if [[Zahirs gäng]] resolved the situation.
