---
title: "Morg Ashbone"
---

**Faction:** [[Ten-Towns]] (Termalaine)

Guard captain of [[Termalaine]].

- Woke [[Zahirs gäng]] in the middle of the night (Days 34–35) and escorted them out of town.
- Accused Borc of the murder of [[Perilou Fishfinger(†)]] — she was found half-naked and dead in the snow outside the city wall.
- Borc panicked at the sight of Morg Ashbone — reason unknown.
- Listened to [[Nasher]]'s explanation about the ghost and the hunt for the real killer. Spoke privately with Borc. Released the party.
