# Session 5 – Bryn Shander & Termalaine

**Day 34 (continued) – Termalaine:**

*Morning Plans:*
- Plan: depart the next morning for [[Bryn Shander]] to investigate Throgga's Import & Goods. Route: Termalaine → Targos (~4 h) → Bryn Shander (~2 h).
- [[Martha]] revealed that Throgga's Import & Goods had operated in Ten-Towns for 15–20 years and stayed behind when other importers fled after the Rime began.

*Midnight Interrogation — Morg Ashbone:*
- In the middle of the night the party was roused by city guards and escorted southeast out of Termalaine.
- The guard captain was waiting outside: **Morg Ashbone**. Borc panicked upon seeing him. Sork created a telepathic link between himself, Nasher, and Borc to calm him — Borc allowed himself to be detained.
- The guards accused Borc of murder. The victim was **[[Perilou Fishfinger]]**. She lay half-naked and dead in the snow outside the city wall, the words **"You owe me"** carved into her chest — murdered by freezing. Someone had stripped her and doused her with cold water until the cold killed her.
- Nasher explained the wraith and the hunt for the real killer to Morg, who eventually accepted the story and spoke with Borc privately. The party was released.
- Searched Perilou's room at the inn — nothing of value beyond 13 gp and 12 sp.

**Day 35 – Targos & Bryn Shander:**

*Sheriff Markham — Sephek Kaltro Revealed:*
- Hired a dog sled and departed for Targos and then Bryn Shander.
- Bryn Shander was packed with refugees — tension and anxiety hung thick inside Town Hall. Sork was nervous and refused to go in to meet the sheriff (Zahir hinted he had once helped Sork leave town during their first week in Icewind Dale).
- **Sheriff Markham** revealed that Throgga's bodyguard was named **Sephek Kaltro** — had appeared roughly 2.5 years ago. The sheriff also told the party about Sork's prior crimes in Bryn Shander. Assigned a city guard escort to accompany them to Throgga's Import & Goods.
- When the party rejoined Sork outside, Nasher gave Sork and Zahir a thorough scolding.

*Throgga's Import & Goods (daytime):*
- Nasher cast Divine Sense outside — sensed nothing.
- Inside: **Kendra** (human, employed five years). Zahir attempted to charm information out of her. Nasher's patience broke: *"YOU'RE UNEMPLOYED. YOUR BOSS IS DEAD."* She did not take it well. Zahir failed to smooth things over and the party was thrown out.
- Nasher tried to get the city guard to arrest Kendra to allow a proper search — refused.

*The Hooked Nucklehead — Hildur:*
- Went to The Hooked Nucklehead to find **[[Hildur Trollbane]]**. Met **Barton** (the owner) — Hildur had been there the day before. Sork and Zahir waited; Nasher and Borc went to the market.
- Bought better armour for Sork and Zahir (sold their old sets). New armour for Nasher/Borc too expensive: splint armour 500 gp, plate armour 2,300 gp, half-plate for Borc 1,200 gp. Also bought two climber's kits.
- **Peonas Elixirs**: run by a female halfling, smelled strongly of roses, copper containers and curiosities. Potion of Speed: 10,000–15,000 gp; also Potions of Featherfall — bought nothing.
- Eventually heard that Hildur had left town 2–3 days earlier and rented a room at **Golden Stag's Rest**.

*Throgga's Import & Goods (night):*
- Zahir spoke with the guard dogs via Speak with Animals and calmed them. Sork picked the front lock; Borc kept watch.
- Nasher, Zahir, and Sork searched the premises: mostly food imports and trade goods — nothing suspicious.
- Sork picked the office lock. Documents showed: **Sephek Kaltro hired three years ago** but extremely well paid since approximately two years and two months back. Throgga's Import & Goods barely broke even — not making real money.
- Nasher triggered a magical lightning trap on a hidden cash box — shocked. Blessed Sork before his attempt — **Sork was also hit by the trap, twice**. Third attempt: the lock opened. Contents: gemstones and a small, beautiful pouch.
- Restored the premises as best they could, locked up, and returned to the inn.

**Day 36 – Back to Termalaine:**

*The Journey and Fieldspar Jewels:*
- With no further leads on Sephek, the party chose to address the new mine in [[Termalaine]]. Dog sled back.
- On arrival, scouted **Fieldspar Jewels** — closed with a sign, nothing notable visible through the window.

*Elka Verryt – Town Hall:*
- Town Hall: large, beautiful stone building with a broad spiral staircase up to the Speaker's office — strangely empty of people.
- **[[Elka Verryt]]** in her office: explained the troubles in the new mine and that the Speaker had not been the one to close it. Convinced the party was there to help.
- The Speaker was at the bathhouse **Sune's Comfort** — entry password: *"Executive Special."*

*Sune's Comfort:*
- Steam and opulent atmosphere. Zahir handled the talking. Receptionist: a female moon elf with very red hair.
- Left all equipment; entered in bathrobes only. Met **Speaker [[Oarus Matthews]]**.
- Oarus believed he had been **drugged or poisoned at The Blue Clam**. Wanted the party's help to reopen the mine.
- Showed them letters from foreman Harroway: the **first letter** demanded closure and that **Jilta Fieldspar** be sent underground. The **second letter** was written as if by someone losing their mind — the handwriting deteriorates noticeably. Suspicious.
- Oarus promised his endorsement if the party resolved the situation. Slept that night at The East Side Inn.

**Day 37 – The New Mine:**

*Chermani Goldgreen — The Old Mine:*
- First visited the old mine to speak with **Chermani Goldgreen**. Learned that **Greg Holdun** was the last to leave the new mine. Three levels. Holdun heard the alarm from the lowest floor and fears Underdark creatures — but this time the alarm had sounded on the **top** floor.

*The Mine Site:*
- Four buildings in a diamond around the mine shaft. Someone had stacked furniture inside the mine ~10 days ago. Fresh footprints: out of the mine, into the forest, and into the foreman's house.

*The Foreman's House:*
- Two-storey building. Ground floor: remains of an abandoned board game mid-session, two bedrooms, laundry room.
- North door on the upper floor: **"I'm sorry"** — Harroway's message to Arno.
- Broke open the blocked door: **Fronda Harroway** hung inside — dead 4–5 days. Suicide. Swollen, glassy eyes; hardened fingernails. Sork found burned papers and a blanket in the corner. Harroway clutched a small bone knot in her hand.

*Arno Rescued:*
- Found a ramshackle hut in the forest. **Arno** lay inside without a pulse — Nasher's Lay on Hands saved him.
- Carried back to a mess-hall building with a fire. No real contact could be established. Zahir and Borc fetched [[Elka Verryt]], who calmed Arno and took him back to town.

*The Mine Site — Disturbing Finds:*
- In the mess hall: **two injured axebeaks, killed by powerful blows to the head**.
- Outside: another axebeak **buried in the ground** — dug down to the bone.
- Everything pointed to the shift workers having been **infected by something, driven mad, and begun eating their own furniture**.
- In the barracks: seven beds, 44 sp and 18 sp. Cooked the axebeak and slept there.
- Restless sleep — an oppressive feeling hung over the mine site all night.
