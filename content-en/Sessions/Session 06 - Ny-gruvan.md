# Session 6 – The New Mine

**Day 38 – Into the Mine:**

*Preparations:*
- Studied the map carefully before entering. Sork took the navigator role and drew his own map as they moved. Initially matched reality — an old cave-in was exactly where noted. The mine felt dark, silent, and malevolent.
- At the entrance: Sork thought he saw a shadow move — found nothing. Zahir set up an alarm spell behind the party. Nasher suspects Zahir was doing something else.
- Along the walls: alarm bells every hundred feet — the old warning system.

*Gror Gersk — Sabotage:*
- Large chamber with rails crisscrossing the floor. Cave-ins scattered. Sork claimed to hear someone chipping stone deeper in — no one else heard anything; the silence that followed felt almost worse than the sound he described.
- In one of the cave-ins: a body — half-orc, dead ~3 weeks. Likely **Gror Gersk** (lieutenant). Zahir believed someone had sabotaged a beam to cause the collapse — Sork noted the chipping stopped the moment they entered that tunnel.

*Kobold Ambush — Nasher's Wings Revealed:*
- Four kobolds lunged from the darkness. Defeated without much trouble.
- Nasher let **his wings emerge and his eyes transform** to terrify a kobold — companions were very surprised.
- Nasher, Borc, and Zahir mined some crystals: ~2 gp worth. Sork rested.

*Gillian Fieldspar — The Lift Shaft:*
- Found a shaft with a wooden deck — remnants of a **bucket lift**. Sork saw a broken lift at the bottom and a body: a **dwarf woman in fine clothes**. Likely **Gillian Fieldspar**.
- Rappelled down with rope and grappling hook. Sork nearly fell but held on.
- Nasher examined the body: died from the fall. But skin and flesh had been **cut from her arms and legs with a blade** — post mortem. Found **two diamonds worth 8 gp each** on Gillian.

*Sork's Paranoia — Nasher's Vision:*
- Sork refused to continue deeper. Eventually admitted: fear of being left behind. He claimed he'd heard the party discussing it — which they hadn't. The mine was getting into his head.
- Nasher received a **vision**: his mother offering a child to an unknown god — the sense that the child was his brother. But Nasher has no brother. The vision vanished as quickly as it came but left something behind.

*The Mushroom Body — Nasher Infected:*
- Sork saw the shadow again behind the party. This time Zahir saw it too. Chased it north through the tunnels.
- Found a tunnel where **mushrooms were growing out of a female body**. No sign of the shadow.
- Zahir came too close and was nearly struck by the mushrooms. Spores spread — Nasher cast Bless to protect the others but inhaled a large amount himself.
- Fast, deep coughing followed by **terrible chest pain** — something had taken hold. Nasher used Lay on Hands on himself to cure the infection.

*Lost in the Mine:*
- Tried to find the way back to the lift shaft. Sork realized the map he'd drawn while chasing the shadow was **worthless** — the party completely lost.
- Retraced their steps one by one: **2.5 hours** before Sork finally found the way back to the lift shaft.
