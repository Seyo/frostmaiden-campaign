# Session 7 – Cadavix

**Day 38 (continued) – The Mine:**

*The Mine Warps Their Sense of Direction:*
- What they had always believed was north turned out to be west. The mine was bending their sense of direction entirely.

*Second Mushroom Body — Borc Infected:*
- Continued south. Found a body hidden among debris — had been there longer than the others, likely since before the mine closed. Perhaps the origin of the infection. Mushrooms growing out of it. Borc came too close, spores spread.
- Nasher checked Borc: seemed okay at first — but the coughing worsened rapidly into terrible chest pain. Nasher was forced to use his **last Lay on Hands** on him.

*Retreat with Gillian's Body:*
- With no Lay on Hands remaining: too dangerous to continue. Decided to return to Termalaine for antidote.
- Brought **Gillian's body** out of respect and so the town could know what had happened.
- Told **[[Elka Verryt]]**: she asked them to leave Gillian at the cemetery but knew nothing about mushrooms in the mine and knew of no healer in Termalaine.
- Termalaine had a tailor, a theatre, and a **Temple of Gond** — no potions for sale.

*To Bryn Shander — Nasher's Dream:*
- The oppressive feeling followed them the whole way — as if something had clung to them and come out of the darkness with them.
- Slept at **Golden Stag's Rest** with **Myrtle**.
- Nasher dreamed again of his mother: standing at an altar, praying in an unknown language. On the altar: a **white skull symbol**. The sense of a dark god — the same god she offered his brother to?

**Day 39:**

*Sork's Breakdown — Fingernails:*
- Bought **3 antitoxins** in Bryn Shander using Myrtle's discount.
- Back to Termalaine and the mine. At the lift shaft: Nasher descended first, then Zahir, then Borc — then **Sork shoved Borc straight into the shaft** and pulled up the rope. Screamed that he was afraid they'd leave him again.
- Borc injured from the fall. Nasher healed him with Healing Word. Climbed back up and chased Sork.
- Found Sork in the foreman's bedroom outside the mine — complete panic, sobbing. Bound in a hogtie. Hysterical, beyond reason. Nasher banged his head on the floor twice; Borc once — beyond help.
- Realized: **their own fingernails had begun to grow and harden** — exactly like Harroway's body still lying in the adjacent room. The magical infection had taken hold in the whole party.

*House of the Triad:*
- Nasher proposed **House of the Triad** in Bryn Shander — they were owed a favor after his preaching in Caer Konig.
- Outside the temple: Nasher overwhelmed by **shame and guilt** — his abandonment of the church in Neverwinter, amplified by the infection. Forced to admit to the others that he was not on the temple's good side — part of why he was up north.
- **Zahir forced them all inside** when they could barely cross the threshold themselves. Seized and put in cages.
- Inside: a **woman in armor** was speaking with the monks — clearly more than a casual visitor.

**Days 40–44:**
- The temple treated the party for **five days**. The magical affliction was lifted. The monks didn't know what it was — only that it was **powerful, terrible, and unnatural**.

**Day 45:**

*The White Skull — The Infection Spreads:*
- Nasher asked the monks about the white skull symbol from his dream — they didn't recognize it. Offered access to the **temple library** for future research.
- Dog sled back toward Termalaine. Halfway to the mine: the oppressive feeling returned — but **spread further now** than five days ago. The infection is moving toward the city. The source must be stopped.

*Hook Horror — Lower Floor:*
- Descended to the lower floor without trouble. Something had **eaten part of the hidden body**. From the darkness: an enormous **hook horror**. Defeated, but Nasher took significant damage. Zahir's Phantasmal Force failed.

*Upper Floor — The Small Creature:*
- Up to the upper floor for the first time. The infection flared — Nasher tripped Zahir (suspicion igniting). Sork stopped the fight: "The mine is trying to turn us against each other."
- Mining shafts heading east — work apparently stopped mid-action, tools left where they lay.
- Sork saw the shadow again. Chased it. Zahir was closest — turned the corner and saw the **creature clearly**: a small, shrunken humanoid with long claws and sharp teeth. It showed itself for a moment, then pulled down a beam — a wall collapsed **onto Zahir**. Unconscious but alive — **one hour to dig him out**.
- Sork tracked the creature down one floor. Zahir set an alarm spell at the lift shaft.

*Tentacle Monster — The Secret Door:*
- Eastern shaft: a **massive tentacle monster reeking of caustic acid**. Dangerous fight. The acid **melted Borc's breastplate** (taken from Nildar) and badly damaged both their weapons. Borc fell unconscious — Nasher cast Healing Word.
- Further east: Sork found a **secret door** with blue light seeping through the cracks. Nasher cast Detect Poison and Disease: **strong necromantic presence** behind the wall — the source of the infection.
- Nasher, Sork, and Zahir took cover. Borc opened the secret door and went in alone.

*The Crystal Egg — The Charm:*
- Borc went silent. Nasher looked out: Borc stood with his back turned, **cutting slices of his own flesh from his arms and eating them** — staring at a **massive blue crystalline egg**.
- Inside the egg: a figure — **a young man with white skin and white hair**. Nearly impossible not to stare. Nasher was charmed too. **Sork grabbed Nasher's cloak** to break him free, then tackled Borc.

> *ARNO MUST DIE. THE SKULL DEMANDS A SACRIFICE. TONIGHT.*
> *(Thoughts planted by the egg's influence — not Nasher's own)*

*Mari Flatstream:*
- The shadow in the chamber was **Mari Flatstream** (lieutenant) — half-transformed into a creature like the one that had tried to kill Zahir with the mine wall. Obsessed with protecting the egg; convinced everything was **Menka Bladeless's** fault.
- Borc tackled her; Nasher bound her — her gaze met Nasher's and **something died in him**. Zahir threw a blanket over her face. Nasher knocked her unconscious.
- In the room: the seven beds the lieutenants had dragged there, **four polished crystals** (200–600 gp each), a **jeweller's kit**. The egg had cracks in it.

*The Egg Opened — High Necromancer Cadavix:*
- Zahir observed the chamber had once been **open to the sky — like a crater** — the egg had come from above.
- Sork and Zahir took cover. Borc struck the egg with one pickaxe blow — it split in two.
- Inside: a **body with translucently white skin**, covered in a layer of **diamond dust**. Skeleton and organs visible through the skin. No signs of life. Around the neck: a **granite plaque** with runes in an unknown language.
- Holy Water — no effect (not undead or fiend). Sork (Draconic) + Nasher (Elvish) identified the language as **Loross** — ancient, related to both, used in the **Netherese Empire** (Zahir knew this).
- The plaque read:

> **High Necromancer Cadavix**
> **(III)**
> **3516 NY**

- Zahir: 3516 NY = **−343 DR**. Current year: 1492 DR → the plaque is **1835 years old**. The body of a high-ranking necromancer from ancient Netheril.

*Cadavix Dropped into the Underdark — The Plan:*
- Zahir's plan: move the body → move the infection. Drop Cadavix into the hole on the first floor and **blast the lower section with smokepowder** — Termalaine can still use the upper levels.
- Nasher mentioned **Dzaan** and the **Arcane Brotherhood** as possible sources of information on Netheril. Borc asked how Nasher knew of them — "It's a long story."
- Sork gathered remaining **diamond dust** from Cadavix. Took crystal shards from the egg. Nasher took the unconscious **Mari Flatstream** (perhaps curable now). Borc carried the body.
- At the hole: **Borc dropped Cadavix into the darkness.** The body fell — but no sound from the bottom ever came. The depths swallowed him whole.
- The oppressive feeling vanished the moment the body fell. The thoughts it had planted remained.
- Plan: buy smokepowder and blast the lower section shut.
