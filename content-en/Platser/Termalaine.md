---
title: "Termalaine"
---

- **Type:** Mining town along the shores of [[Maer Dualdon]], Ten-Towns.
- **Population:** Approx. 600.
- **Character:** Sacrifices warmth to [[Auril]] — freezes willingly. Mines crystals and diamonds.

**Key Locations:**
- **The East Side Inn:** Interconnected buildings via underground tunnels. Innkeeper [[Martha]], close friend of [[Keegan Valryn]] and [[Garret]]. No open fires allowed in the rooms.
- **The Blue Clam:** Tavern. [[Oarus Matthews|Speaker Oarus Matthews]] was last seen here — got drunk and disappeared.
- **Sune's Comfort:** Bathhouse. [[Oarus Matthews|Speaker Oarus Matthews]] hid here (Day 36). Password: *"Executive Special"*.
- **The Graveyard:** Maintained by **Crank** (gnome). Murder victims are kept here.
- **New Mine:** Closed for ~2 weeks (Days 29–33) — monsters came up from underground. Investigated by [[Zahirs gäng]] on Days 37–45. See [[#The New Mine]] below.
- **Old Mine:** Nearly depleted.
- **Fieldspar:** Dwarf jewellery shop, owned by [[Gillian Fieldspar(†)]]. Closed since ~Day 26. Gillian found dead in the new mine on Day 38.

**Key People:**
- **[[Oarus Matthews|Speaker Oarus Matthews]]** — town leader. Went missing on Day 33, found at Sune's Comfort on Day 36. Confirmed the troubles with the new mine.
- **[[Elka Verryt]]** — Oarus's secretary. Confirmed the troubles.
- **[[Morg Ashbone]]** — Guard captain. Accused Borc of Perilou's murder (Days 34–35), released the party after their explanation.
- **Heskam Wyrmhide** — silver-scaled dragonborn, lives in the mine workers' collective.
- **[[Arno]]** — mute boy, orphaned (Tribe of the Wolf), adopted by mine foreman [[Fronda Harroway(†)]]. Found without a pulse in the forest outside the new mine on Day 37 — saved by [[Nasher]]'s Lay on Hands.

**The Murders:**
- **Day 15:** A human fisherman was found with a snowflake carved into his chest (reported during the rest on Days 19–23). [[Garret]] and [[The Burning Hammers]] were heading here but were attacked at [[Kelvins Cairn]].
- **Bromm Runehand** (~Day 25) — found at the harbour, first victim in Termalaine.
- **Fjordis Jewelhand** (~Day 30) — mine worker. Stab and slash wounds + snowflake frost-burned into the left shoulder.
- **[[Perilou Fishfinger(†)|Perilou Fishfinger]]** (Days 34–35) — found half-naked and dead in the snow outside the city wall, **"You owe me"** carved into her chest. Murdered by freezing. Borc was falsely accused.
- The murders coincided with [[Throgga Icevein(†)|Throgga's Import & Goods]] deliveries here. See [[Morden i Ten-Towns]].

## The New Mine

Investigated by [[Zahirs gäng]] on Days 37–45.

**Staff:**
- **[[Fronda Harroway(†)|Foreman Fronda Harroway]]** (dwarf) — found hanged in the foreman's house on Day 37, suicide (4–5 days old). Letters showed signs of severe mental deterioration.
- **Gror Gersk** (lieutenant, half-orc) — found dead in a cave-in, likely deliberate sabotage of a support beam.
- **[[Mari Flatstream]]** (lieutenant, dwarf) — half-transformed by the crystalline egg, obsessed with protecting it.
- **[[Gillian Fieldspar(†)]]** — found dead at the bottom of the elevator shaft; flesh cut away from arms and legs post mortem.
- Remaining lieutenants (Menka Bladeless, Oval Chon, Brin Ruk'Natsk, Bezeuth von Deeb, Sod Suscagan) — fate unknown.

**Findings and Events:**
- Kobold attack inside the mine — defeated.
- Fungal bodies — spores caused a magical infection (nails hardened and grew). Borc was infected on Day 38.
- **Hook horror** — defeated on the lowest level.
- **Hidden wall with blue light** — strong necrotic presence behind it. Borc tore it open alone.
- **Crystalline Egg** — blue necrotic relic. Borc and Nasher were charmed by the egg. Sork pulled them both free.
- **[[Cadavix(†)|High Necromancer Cadavix]]** — found sealed inside the egg. Body with translucent white skin, diamond dust. The plaque read: 3516 NY = −343 DR — **1,835 years old** (Netherese, Loross language). Borc threw Cadavix into the darkness (Day 45); no sound was heard from below. The oppressive feeling in the mine vanished.
- **The infection** (hardened nails, hallucinations) spread further toward the town by Day 45. The party was treated at [[Bryn Shander]] House of the Triad for 5 days (Days 40–44) — the monks only knew it was powerful, terrible, and unnatural.
