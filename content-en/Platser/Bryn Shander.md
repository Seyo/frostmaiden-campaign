---
title: "Bryn Shander"
---

- **Type:** Largest city in Ten-Towns, crowded with refugees.
- **Key locations:**
    - *The Hooked Knucklehead:* Inn where the party rested for a week. Hildur Trollbane had left the city by the party's second visit — at Golden Stag's Rest they learned she had headed south.
    - *House of the Triad:* Temple. The party was locked in cages on Day 39 (Zahir forced them in; Nasher confessed to leaving the church). Treated for 5 days against the necrotic infection (Days 40–44) — the monks only knew it was powerful, terrible, and unnatural.
    - *Reindil's Emporium:* High-end gear, run by a half-elf.
    - *Council Hall:* Home of the sheriff.
    - *Sheriff Markham:* Told the party that Sephek Kaltro (Throgga's bodyguard) had appeared ~2.5 years ago and was extremely well-paid for the past 2 years and 2 months. Also mentioned Sork's crimes in Bryn Shander.
- All of [[Zahirs gäng]] are on the sacrifice list.
- **Throgga's Import and Goods:** [[Throgga Icevein(†)]]'s trading company, operating out of here. Throgga was revealed as undead in Termalaine on Day 34 — actually dead for 2.5 years. The company was used as cover for the serial killers. Searched by the party at night (Day 35): a strongbox with gemstones and a small ornate pouch — Nasher and Sork were hit by a magical trap. Sephek Kaltro was hired 3 years ago.
